# Viralyst

AI-powered virality simulation platform that models how content propagates through a network of synthetic personas before publication.

Viralyst simulates audience behavior, engagement decisions, algorithmic amplification, and content diffusion to provide creators, marketers, and researchers with insights into the potential performance of a piece of content.

---

## Overview

Social media platforms only provide analytics after content has been published.

Viralyst explores a different approach:

> What if we could simulate thousands of audience members and estimate how content spreads before posting it?

The platform creates AI personas with different demographics, interests, and behavioral traits. These personas interact with uploaded content and decide whether to watch, like, comment, share, or skip it.

The resulting interactions generate a propagation network that can be analyzed to understand reach, engagement, audience crossover, and virality potential.

## Features

### Content Upload

* Upload short-form video content
* Preview uploaded media before simulation

### AI Persona Simulation

* Synthetic users with unique demographics and interests
* Different engagement behaviors
* In-target and out-of-target audience modeling

### Engagement Modeling

Personas can:

* Watch
* Like
* Comment
* Share
* Skip

Each action contributes to the content propagation process.

### Propagation Network Visualization

* Interactive network graph
* Content diffusion paths
* In-target vs out-of-target audience visualization
* Share-driven and algorithm-driven exposure

### Persona Inspection

Click any simulated persona to view:

* Demographics
* Interests
* Followers
* Watch percentage
* Engagement action
* Reasoning behind behavior

### Virality Verdict Engine

Generates high-level outcomes such as:

* Viral Hit
* Niche Hit
* Echo Chamber
* Needs Rework

along with supporting metrics.

## Inspiration

This project was inspired by emerging ideas around AI personas, recommendation systems, content propagation modeling, and virality simulation.

The goal of Viralyst is not to replicate any specific platform but to explore how synthetic audiences can be used to better understand content performance and engagement dynamics.

---

## Disclaimer

Viralyst provides simulated insights and comparative analysis.

Results should be interpreted as behavioral simulations rather than real-world predictions of social media performance.
